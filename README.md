## 📖 Description

**FR** – Moteur de trading quantitatif crypto en Rust pur. 6 modules : collecte multi‑exchange, 128 features, **Mixture of Experts** (256 experts), risque (VaR, drawdown 3%), exécution microsecondes, réinvestissement automatique + staking.

**EN** – Pure Rust crypto quant engine. 6 modules: multi‑exchange collection, 128 features, **Mixture of Experts** (256 experts), risk (VaR, drawdown 3%), microsecond execution, auto reinvestment + staking.

**ES** – Motor cuántico crypto en Rust puro. 6 módulos: recolección multi‑exchange, 128 features, **Mixture of Experts** (256 expertos), riesgo (VaR, drawdown 3%), ejecución microsegundos, reinversión automática + staking.
